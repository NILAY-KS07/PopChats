Community Rules

Keep conversations open, respectful, and safe.

PopChats is designed for lightweight realtime communication. These guidelines help maintain a better experience for everyone.

Respectful Behavior

  • No harassment, threats, or targeted abuse
  • No racism, hate speech, or discriminatory content
  • Respect other users and conversations

Privacy & Safety

  • No doxxing or sharing private information
  • Do not impersonate other individuals or organizations
  • Avoid sharing sensitive personal data publicly

Spam & Abuse

  • No spam, flooding, or malicious links
  • No automated abuse or platform exploitation
  • No illegal or intentionally harmful activity

Moderation

PopChats may temporarily restrict users, rooms, or activity patterns that negatively impact platform stability or community safety.

These measures help maintain a lightweight and usable realtime environment for everyone.
