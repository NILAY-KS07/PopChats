PopChats

Privacy Policy

PopChats is built around temporary and lightweight communication. We do not require account creation, passwords, or personal profiles.

What We Store

PopChats does not permanently store messages, chat history, or user accounts. Rooms are disposable and designed for temporary communication.

Security & Abuse Prevention

To help protect the platform from spam, abuse, and attacks, limited technical information such as IP addresses may be temporarily processed for rate limiting and security monitoring.

Cookies & Sessions

PopChats uses temporary session cookies for authentication, CSRF protection, and maintaining active chat sessions.

Third-Party Services

PopChats may use third-party infrastructure or CDN services required for realtime communication and website delivery.

Data Persistence

PopChats is intentionally designed without long-term message persistence. Conversations are not intended to be stored permanently.

Changes

This privacy policy may change as the platform evolves.
