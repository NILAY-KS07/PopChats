Privacy

Privacy designed around temporary communication.

PopChats is built to provide lightweight realtime conversations without accounts, public identities, or unnecessary data collection.

What We Store

PopChats does not require account creation, passwords, or personal profiles.

Messages and rooms are designed to be temporary. PopChats is not intended to function as a permanent social platform or long-term messaging archive.

Sessions & Cookies

Temporary cookies or session technologies may be used to maintain active chat functionality and help provide a stable user experience while using the platform.

Security & Platform Protection

Limited technical information may be processed temporarily to help protect PopChats from spam, abuse, automated traffic, and malicious activity.

These measures are intended solely to maintain platform stability, availability, and safety.

Third-Party Services

PopChats may rely on third-party infrastructure, hosting, or content delivery providers required for realtime communication and platform delivery.

Data Persistence

PopChats is intentionally designed around temporary communication. Conversations are not intended to be permanently stored or archived.

Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated over time as PopChats evolves, improves, or introduces new features.
