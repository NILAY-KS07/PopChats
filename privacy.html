Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

At PopChats, we take your privacy seriously. This platform is designed to be truly anonymous and ephemeral.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect or store any of the following:

  • Your real name, email address, or phone number.
  • Your physical location.
  • Chat logs or message history after a session ends.
  • Browser cookies for tracking or advertising.

2. Data Usage

We only use minimal data to ensure the service works:

  • Usernames: Stored temporarily in an SQLite database to ensure uniqueness.
  • IP Address: Used in memory for 2-second rate-limiting to prevent spam. We do not log these permanently.
  • Local Storage: Used to save your username locally so you can stay in the chat room during your session.

3. Author & Credits

PopChats was conceived and created by N K Shrivastava. The project was further refined and secured with the assistance of his friend to ensure a safe environment for all users.

4. Security

We implement modern security practices, including XSS protection and rate-limiting, to keep the chat environment safe from malicious scripts and spam.
